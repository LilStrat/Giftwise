When you're apart, the best gifts are the ones that make you feel present: something they use every day that reminds them of you, or something you do together on a call. Plan early, because shipping to another city or country takes longer than you think.

## Things that keep you in their day

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**Touch lamps that light up together.** When one of you taps your lamp, the other lamp glows. A quiet way to say "thinking of you" across time zones. Both lamps need WiFi.

</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A dual time zone clock or watch.** Shows their time and yours, so they always know when it's a good moment to call.

</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**Open when letters.** A bundle of sealed letters labelled "open when you miss me", "open when you can't sleep" and so on. It costs only time, and gets reread for years.

</div>

## Things you do together, apart

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A matching dinner kit for a video date.** Send the same meal kit, wine or dessert to both homes, then cook and eat together on a call.
{% include amz.html q="date night box for couples" %}
</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A two player online game or a shared puzzle.** Pick a game you can both play from your own devices, and set a regular game night.
</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A couples question book.** Read a page each on your calls. It gives you new things to talk about after months of "how was your day".
{% include amz.html q="couples question book" %}
</div>

## Something for the next visit

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**Travel booked, or a fund towards it.** The best gift is the next time you see each other. If tickets aren't possible yet, a printed countdown with a planned date works too.
</div>

## Quick tips

- Ship to them by early February, and allow extra time for international post.
- Agree a time for a Valentine's call that works in both time zones.
- A small gift on the day plus a big plan for the next visit feels better than one big parcel.

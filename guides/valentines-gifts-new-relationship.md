Early on, the goal is to show you've been listening without making things weird. Small, personal and shareable beats big and expensive. Think about something they mentioned on a date, and build around that.

## Something you do together

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A planned date.** Book a table somewhere they mentioned wanting to try, or a cooking class, or a comedy night. Plans show effort without the pressure of a big object.
</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">

## Things to skip for now

- Jewellery, perfume or clothing. Too personal, and sizes are a guess.
- Anything with both your names or photos on it.
- Big ticket items. They can make the other person feel they owe you.

## Quick tips

- Agree on expectations if you can. A simple "are we doing gifts?" is fine.
- A handwritten card is the part they'll keep.
- If you're unsure, an experience you share is always the safest choice.

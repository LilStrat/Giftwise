Kids love Valentine's Day for the cards and treats, but you can make it about more than a sugar rush. A small gift, a note in their lunchbox, or an afternoon making something together goes a long way.

## Small gifts from parents

<div class="pick" markdown="1">

<div class="pick" markdown="1">

## Something to do together

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**Bake heart shaped biscuits.** A heart cookie cutter set and some icing turns a weekend afternoon into a Valentine's activity, and the results become gifts for others.
{% include amz.html q="heart cookie cutter set" %}

## Quick tips

- Ask the teacher how many children are in the class and whether there are allergy rules.
- Help younger kids write names on cards over a few evenings, not all in one go.
- A short note in their lunchbox on the day costs nothing and makes their morning.

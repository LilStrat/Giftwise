Teens are hard to buy for because their tastes change fast and they hate anything that looks like you tried too hard. Useful tech, things that give them independence, and experiences with friends usually land. When in doubt, ask a sibling or friend what they've mentioned lately.

## If they're into music or gaming

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**Wireless earbuds.** Used every single day, on the bus, at the gym and in bed. Check which phone they have so the earbuds pair easily.

<div class="pick" markdown="1">

## If they'd rather do something

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**Tickets for them and a friend.** A concert, a match, an escape room or a theme park day. Wrap the printed tickets in a card so there's something to open.
</div>

## Quick tips

- Gift cards are fine for teens. Pair one with a small physical gift so it doesn't feel like an afterthought.
- Avoid clothing with obvious branding unless you know exactly what they like.
- Order by early December. Popular tech sells out close to Christmas.

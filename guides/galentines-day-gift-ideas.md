<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A care package by post.** Their favourite snacks, a handwritten letter and a small treat. Post it a week early so it arrives in time.
</div>

## Quick tips

- Set a group budget if you're swapping gifts, so nobody feels awkward.
- Book restaurants early. February 13 and 14 fill up fast.
- A card listing your favourite memories together beats anything you can buy.

Most grandparents will tell you the best gift is seeing the grandkids. So lean into that: gifts that carry the family into their home, make everyday life a bit easier, or give them time with you.

## Gifts that bring the family to them

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A photo book of the year.** Pull the best pictures of the grandkids from your phone and have them printed and bound. Add short captions with dates and what was happening.

## Things to do and enjoy

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A jigsaw puzzle of a family photo.** Turn a favourite picture into a puzzle. It's a gift and an afternoon activity in one.
{% include amz.html q="custom photo jigsaw puzzle" %}
</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**Large print puzzle books.** Crosswords, word searches or sudoku in large print keep them busy through the quiet days after Christmas.

</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A planned day with the grandkids.** A lunch, a garden centre trip or a museum visit, with the date already set. Wrap a printed card so there's something to open.
</div>

## Quick tips

- Ask what they're struggling with lately. Small fixes often beat big gifts.
- Set up any tech before giving it, and write simple instructions on a card.
- Get the grandkids involved in wrapping or making a card. That's the part they'll treasure.

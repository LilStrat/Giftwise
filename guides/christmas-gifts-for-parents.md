"We don't need anything" usually means "we don't want clutter." The trick is to give something they'll use up, use daily, or remember. Pick the section that sounds like them.

## If they love being at home

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A digital photo frame you load from your phone.** You and the rest of the family can send new pictures to it all year, so it never goes stale. Look for one that supports an app or email upload so they never have to touch a cable.
{% include amz.html q="wifi digital picture frame app" %}
</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A heated throw blanket.** It gets used every evening from November to March, and it means they can keep the thermostat lower. Pick one with an auto shut off timer.
{% include amz.html q="heated throw blanket auto shut off" %}
</div>

## If they're always in the kitchen

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A family recipe book, printed.** Collect the dishes they cook, plus a few from siblings and grandparents, and have it bound. It costs an evening of typing and becomes the gift they show visitors.
{% include amz.html q="blank recipe book binder" %}
</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A good chef's knife and a sharpener.** Many parents are still using the knife block from their wedding. An 8 inch chef's knife covers almost every job.
{% include amz.html q="8 inch chef knife" %}
</div>

## If they'd rather spend time than own things

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A planned day out, on you.** Tickets to a show, a nice lunch, or a day trip, with the date already agreed. Wrap a printed card with the plan so there's something to open.
</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A story recorder or "tell me your story" journal.** A guided book of questions about their childhood and early years. Ask them to fill it in, or sit with them and record the answers.
{% include amz.html q="tell me your story journal for parents" %}
</div>

## If they're slowing down a bit

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A smart speaker set up for them.** Timers, reminders, weather and calling family by voice. Set it up before you wrap it, and add your contacts.
{% include amz.html q="smart speaker" %}
</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A long handled reacher or a set of jar openers.** Practical rather than sentimental, so pair it with something fun. Useful when hands or knees start to complain.
{% include amz.html q="electric jar opener" %}
</div>

## Quick tips

- Ask a sibling what they've complained about lately. That's your shortlist.
- Consumables (good coffee, olive oil, a wine club) fit parents who hate clutter.
- Order by early December. Shipping slows down fast after the second week.

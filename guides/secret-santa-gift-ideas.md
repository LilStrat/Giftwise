Secret Santa is hard because you often draw someone you know one fact about. Work from that one fact. Stick to the group's spending limit and skip anything too personal, like clothing sizes or perfume.

## If they're a coffee or tea person

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A temperature control mug or an insulated tumbler.** Desk drinks go cold within minutes. A good tumbler fixes that and gets used every working day.
{% include amz.html q="insulated coffee tumbler with lid" %}
</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A tea or coffee sampler.** Lets them try a few kinds without committing. Easy to wrap and no one has too much of it.
{% include amz.html q="tea sampler gift box" %}
</div>

## If they're always at their desk

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A desk plant that's hard to kill.** A small succulent or snake plant in a nice pot. Low effort, and it makes a grey office look better.
{% include amz.html q="small succulent plant in pot" %}
</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A wireless charging pad.** Neutral, useful, and fits almost any phone made in the last few years.
{% include amz.html q="wireless charging pad" %}
</div>

## If you know nothing at all about them

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A nice candle.** The classic safe pick for a reason. Choose a soft, seasonal scent like pine or vanilla rather than anything strong.
{% include amz.html q="soy candle pine scent" %}
</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**A card or party game for the group.** Something they can bring to their own holiday gatherings. Games with short rounds and simple rules suit the widest crowd.
{% include amz.html q="party card game for adults" %}
</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
**Gourmet snacks.** A box of good chocolate or a snack sampler gets eaten, not stored. Check the group chat for allergies first.
{% include amz.html q="gourmet chocolate gift box" %}
</div>

## Quick tips

- Write a short note with the gift. It's the part people remember.
- Include a gift receipt in case it misses.
- If the group has a theme (white elephant, handmade, under a limit), follow it exactly.
